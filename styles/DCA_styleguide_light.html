The visual system for Digital Custody Academy: professional education in institutional-grade digital asset custody and security. Academy navy surfaces, lowercase Poppins headlines that end in a blue full stop, and Space Mono body copy. Use this as the single source of truth when deriving new assets. Values mirror tailwind.config.ts and client/global.css.

01 · The Academy

secure. verified.
professional.

Digital Custody Academy (DCA) teaches the teams who hold other people's keys: board members, compliance officers, security engineers and custody operators. The curriculum is aligned to recognised standards (CCSS, CMTA DACS, NIST CSF) and leads to verified credentials.

DCA is an Enigma Technologies Solutions initiative. It shares the parent's type system (Poppins × Space Mono) and its "cryptography, made legible" directness. Where ETS uses black and volt, DCA uses navy and a calm blue, because an academy that certifies custody professionals has to read as institutional, steady and trustworthy.

TaglineSecure. Verified. Professional.

DescriptorProfessional Education in Digital Asset Security

Voice · do

  • Plain, precise, standards-literate. Name the framework, the control, the role.
  • Say who a course is for ("Ideal for: compliance officers, operations leads").
  • Short declaratives. "Learn at your own pace."

Voice · don't

  • Hype, price talk or "to the moon" crypto slang.
  • Fear-selling about hacks and losses.
  • Unverifiable claims about credentials or accreditation.

02 · Logo

The DCA tile.

The mark is DCA in a bold slab-monospace, centred in a square tile (1:1). Unlike the 3:2 ETS badge, the DCA tile is always square. It comes in two colourways, both shipped in /public: the navy tile (dca-logo.png) for light grounds and the white tile (dca-logo-white.png) for navy grounds. Next to the tile, the name is set in Space Mono, with the ETS endorsement line underneath.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space of at least ¼ of the tile width on every side (hatched). Minimum size: 32 px on screen (the site header uses 40 px, h-10 w-10) and 10 mm in print. At UI sizes the tile may take a 4 px corner radius (rounded). Never make it more rounded than that.

Rules

  • Navy tile on white or grey-light; white tile on navy or deep navy.
  • Always use the supplied PNGs. Don't re-typeset "DCA".
  • In a lockup, the tile height matches the two-line wordmark block.
  • Don't crop the tile to a circle or a 3:2 rectangle.
  • Don't recolour it to Custody Blue or add gradients.
  • Don't put the navy tile on navy. Switch to the white tile.
  • Don't drop the ETS endorsement from full lockups on marketing surfaces.

07 · Lesson Content

reading mode.

Lessons are long reads, so the reading surface is white with Text Dark ink, and navy appears only in table heads and code blocks. The live styles are in .lesson-content in client/global.css. This is a reference rendering with sample content, not curriculum.

Cryptographic Provenance

Provenance answers a simple question: who signed this, and has it changed since? A detached signature over a hash lets an auditor check both without trusting the channel it came through. See the CCSS key-generation controls.

Why custody depends on it

A signature proves possession of a key, not the identity of a person. Identity comes from the key ceremony.

Run sha256sum over the artefact, then verify:

$ openssl dgst -sha256 -verify pub.pem \
    -signature report.sig report.pdf
Verified OK

Imagery

  • Prefer typography and diagrams over photography.
  • If you use photos, pick cool-toned, desaturated shots of infrastructure, hardware or people at work, under a navy overlay at 60–80%.
  • Diagrams use Academy Navy strokes, a Custody Blue highlight and Space Mono labels.

Don't

  • Glowing coins, padlocks, hooded hackers or other stock-crypto clichés.
  • Framework logos arranged as a "trusted by" or partner wall.
  • ETS volt yellow on DCA surfaces. The two palettes stay separate.
