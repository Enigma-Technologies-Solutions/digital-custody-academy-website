Digital Custody Academy is run by Enigma Technologies Solutions Limited, Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates ("we"). This page covers this website and the Digital Custody Academy newsletter. Questions and requests go to [jjpa@digitalcustody.academy](mailto:jjpa@digitalcustody.academy).

## Visiting the site

The site has no analytics, no advertising and no cookies of its own. Like any web page, it is served by companies that see your IP address and browser details when it loads:

- **GitHub Pages** (GitHub, Inc.) hosts the site.
- **Google Fonts** (Google LLC) serves the typefaces.

## The newsletter

When you subscribe, we collect your **email address** and which list you chose. We use it only to send you the Digital Custody Academy newsletter: new modules and lessons, and new posts from the blog. We do not sell it, share it with anyone else, or add it to other lists.

How sign-up works:

1. When you start typing in the form, the page loads **Cloudflare Turnstile**, a check that the form is filled in by a person. Cloudflare processes your IP address and browser signals for that check.
2. When you submit, our sign-up service on **Cloudflare Workers** checks the request and asks **Resend** to email you a confirmation link. Your IP address is used for rate limiting and is not stored. Our logs record that a sign-up happened, never your address.
3. You are only subscribed once you open the link and press Confirm. If you do not, nothing is added to the list. Resend keeps its usual record of the confirmation email it sent.

Once you confirm, your address is stored with **Resend** (Resend, Inc.), which sends the newsletter for us. We do not track whether you open our emails or click their links.

## Unsubscribing and deletion

Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link, which takes effect immediately. After you unsubscribe, Resend keeps your address marked as unsubscribed so that you are not emailed again.

To have your address deleted entirely, or to ask what we hold about you, write to [jjpa@digitalcustody.academy](mailto:jjpa@digitalcustody.academy). We reply within 30 days.
